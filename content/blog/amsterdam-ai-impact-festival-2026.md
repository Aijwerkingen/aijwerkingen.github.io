On 24 November the [AI Impact Festival](https://amsterdamai.com/nl/events/amsterdam-ai-impact-festival-2026/) returned to the NEMO Science Museum in Amsterdam for its second edition. One afternoon, one question: what does AI actually mean for your life?

The programme worked through the places AI already sits. How the city of Amsterdam uses it, and for whom. What it does to your sense of authorship at work. How it shapes creativity, and what it does to public conversation. Alongside that ran a session on AI and mental wellbeing: can AI make us feel better?

That session, given by Evi-Anne van Dis of Amsterdam UMC, covered the register we are building. The festival's own description of it is the plainest summary:

> ... an AI Register being developed to better understand mental health problems that people suspect are linked to AI applications.

## Why a register

When a medicine causes an unexpected effect, there is somewhere to report it, and those reports accumulate into evidence that researchers and regulators can act on. When a conversational AI tool or a social media platform leaves someone more anxious, more isolated, or unable to stop, there is usually nowhere for that to go. It stays a private experience, and nothing aggregates. That is the gap the register exists to close.

Two things follow from taking that seriously. Reports have to be collected from anyone, without an account and without a name, because the people who notice these effects are not a special group. And they have to be collected in a form researchers can actually study, so that patterns show up rather than anecdotes.

## What comes next

The register is in development, and the reporting form is launching soon. It will live [here](/report). We are working with researchers on how reports are read, how they are stored, and what they can and cannot be used to claim.

If you have noticed something in yourself, or in someone you are close to, that you suspect is connected to an AI tool, that is exactly the kind of experience the register is for. When it opens, it will take a few minutes to add.
