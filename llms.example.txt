# AIjwerkingen

> AIjwerkingen is a public service for reporting perceived adverse effects of
> conversational AI tools and digital/social media. Anyone affected, and
> parents, carers, and professionals supporting someone else, can submit
> reports to help identify patterns of harm. This is not a crisis or
> emergency service; in an emergency, contact your local emergency number.

## Key facts
- Purpose: collect perceived adverse-effect reports about conversational AI tools and
  digital/social media platforms, from the public and professionals.
- Data handling: anonymous by default, data minimised, EU data residency, encrypted at rest.
- Not a diagnosis, treatment, or moderation-enforcement service.
