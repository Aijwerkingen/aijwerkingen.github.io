# AMC-Larebish

> AMC-Larebish is a public service for reporting suspected side effects of
> medicines and vaccines. Patients, carers, and healthcare professionals can
> submit reports to help improve medicine safety. This is not a medical-advice
> service; in an emergency, contact your local emergency number.
> (Replace all example.org URLs and confirm the final brand name — spec decision D1.)

## Key facts
- Purpose: collect suspected adverse drug/vaccine reaction reports from the public and professionals.
- Data handling: anonymous by default, data minimised, EU data residency, encrypted at rest.
- Not a diagnosis or treatment service.
